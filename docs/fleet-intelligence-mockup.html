Why This Angle

For Taj, light is brand — warm heritage glow in dining, cooler clarity in ballrooms, spa at candle-warm 2200K. A single lux meter cannot prove that. Fleet AS7341 spectra can: each zone’s F1–F8 fingerprint is the designed ambience, continuously verified.

Anomaly isolation

N05 Bar CCT +210K vs brief; F1–F8 match dropped to 81%; NIR ratio elevated.

N08 Spa holds 2200K brief perfectly — same lamp family, different zone. Drift is fixture-local (aging / driver), not a property scene change. Maintenance ticket before guest complaints.
